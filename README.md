## 🔍 Key Insights
- **Top Reason for Leaving is Better Opportunity:** 50.81% employees (312 out of 614) left the company for better opportunities and work culture.
- **High Attrition Rate:** Out of 835 total employees, 614 have left, which is 73.5% attrition - a major concern for HR.
- **Salary & Growth Factor:** Despite an average salary of ₹80,906 and 12.26 years average experience, employees are still leaving for Better Salary and Career Growth.
- **Work-Life Balance Issue:** Employees with overtime and low job satisfaction have a higher rate of leaving.
